Our Story

It all started in a place of silence—when the weight of loneliness and alcohol dependency felt like a heavy storm cloud over me. There were days I couldn’t see past the dark, and I thought I was trapped.

But in that moment, when everything seemed lost, I found music. Music was the small spark that cut through the chaos. It gave me peace. It gave me a voice.

There were nights I felt so lost, staring at the ceiling, and it was music that kept me afloat. Each note was a step toward hope. And slowly, the storm inside me began to clear.

Today, I stand here with a clear heart and a strong spirit, saying: Alcohol is never the answer. Every song I create now is a testament to survival, to resilience, and to the belief that we can rebuild ourselves, one step at a time.

If you’re reading this, and you feel like the storm inside is too strong, please know: you are never alone. Reach out, speak to someone. Your story is not over.
